## What You Can Steal
- **Open with the viewer's lived experience, not your thesis.** Start with the specific situation they've felt ("If you dress well…") and let the argument emerge from the pattern. Never open with "Here's why people judge you."
- **Build a stacked list before you resolve it.** Three to five escalating examples create tension that a single example can't. The list is the engine; the resolution is the payoff.
- **End on a portable line.** Write your last sentence as if it will be screenshotted alone, with no context. If it doesn't survive on its own, rewrite it until it does.
