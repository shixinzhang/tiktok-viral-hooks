## What You Can Steal
1. **Use "You wanna…?" in the first 3 seconds to pull viewers into the scene** — don't explain the product first, create a "happening now" sense of presence.

3. **Use short sentences + action rhythm to drive momentum** (Sit. Boom. Ready? Go.), boosting completion rate; end with an intimate, cute line to soften the commercial feel and stimulate sharing.
