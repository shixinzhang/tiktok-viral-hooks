## Why It Spreads

- **Moral ambiguity**: Neither spouse is a clean hero. Comment sections split between "Busy is a queen" and "Busy is terrifying" — engagement bait built into the script.

## What You Can Steal
- **Open mid-confession, not mid-setup.** Drop the viewer into the worst sentence of the story in the first 3 seconds. No "so this happened yesterday…" preamble.
- **Withhold the reaction the audience expects.** When everyone anticipates screaming, deliver silence. The discomfort of a missing emotion is stickier than the emotion itself.
- **End on the antagonist's fear, not the protagonist's victory.** Flip POV in the final beat so the audience leaves with a question ("What is she planning?") instead of a resolution — that question is what fuels comments, stitches, and part 2.
