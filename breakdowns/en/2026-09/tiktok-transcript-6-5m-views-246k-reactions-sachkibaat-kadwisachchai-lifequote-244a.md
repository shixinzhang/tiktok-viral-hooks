## What You Can Steal
- **1. Universal Identity Hook:** In the first line, state a trait that the viewer can claim as their own ("The person who speaks the truth...", "The girl who endures everything...") — instant self-insert.
- **2. Enemy List Technique:** List 3–4 concrete places/people (family, office, friends) — don't speak in abstractions. This way, everyone finds themselves in at least one of them.
- **3. Inverted Truth Ending:** In the last line, give the opposite of expectation — where good people suffer and bad people are rewarded. Keep it non-resolved, so that arguments break out in the comments — that's the reach.
