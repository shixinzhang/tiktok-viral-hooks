## What You Can Steal
1. **Open with a dichotomous question, not an introduction.** The first sentence = a choice between two things the audience already feels (love/fear, money/freedom, talent/work). Zero context before the question.
2. **Prove the claim with a scene, not an explanation.** Instead of "leaders should inspire awe," show a micro-situation ("I make a joke, everyone laughs — and I'm not that funny"). One concrete image > three abstract claims.
3. **Build in a twist before the last sentence.** Set an expectation ("fear is better"), then add nuance to it ("but not hatred"). The twist is what gets quoted and shared — without it, the video is just provocation.
