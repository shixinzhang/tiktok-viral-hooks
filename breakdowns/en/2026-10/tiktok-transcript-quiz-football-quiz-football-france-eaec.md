## Why it spreads

## What you can steal
- **Open with a question that has an instant answer.** Don't let the viewer think for more than 2 seconds. Question → answer → next question. Rhythm beats depth.
- **End with a subjective question with no answer.** Not a factual quiz — an identity debate. "Who is the best?" generates 10x more comments than "Who won in 2014?"
- **Slip the CTAs between segments, not at the end.** "Save the video" after the 3rd question, "subscribe" after the 5th. The viewer acts before realizing they were asked.
