## What You Can Steal
1. **Open with the accusation, not the lesson.** Start your video with the exact judgment your audience has received ("People say you're lazy if…") — recognition beats introduction.
2. **Stack binary contrasts to widen your audience.** Each "if X, they say Y / if not-X, they say Z" pair recruits a new segment. 4–6 pairs is the sweet spot before fatigue.
3. **Land on a permission line, not a moral.** End with "don't ruin your peace" / "the decision is yours" — give the viewer a phrase they can repeat, caption, or send to someone.
