## Mind Map

# Post-Cesarean Delivery Care Guide

## Importance of Post-Cesarean Care
### Critical Recovery Period
- At least 45 days of proper self-care
- Baby care is essential, but self-care must not be neglected
### Risks of Neglecting Self-Care
- Back pain
- Abdominal pain
- Stitch infection

## Preventing Constipation
### Why It Matters
- Constipation causes straining during bowel movements
- Straining puts pressure on stitches
- Risk of stitches opening or other complications
### Action
- Avoid constipation at all costs

## Understanding Stitch Healing Timeline
### External Stitches
- Take 40–45 days to heal
### Internal Stitches
- Take 3 months or more to heal
### Action
- Care for stitches properly
- Follow doctor's instructions on antibiotics

## Safe Movement & Bending
### Getting Up from Bed
- Avoid sudden jerky movements
- Take help from someone if possible
- If alone, bend knees slightly before rising
### Bathing in First Week
- Take help from mother or family member
- If alone, use a bucket on a stool to pour water
- Avoid bending down too much

## Lifting & Physical Strain
### Weight Restrictions
- Avoid lifting heavy objects
- Can lift the baby (only baby's weight)
### Avoid Straining
- Coughing, sneezing, or laughing puts pressure on stitches
- Never control urination — it strains stitches

## Intimacy After Delivery
### Waiting Period
- No sexual relations for at least 40 days

## Mental & Emotional Well-being
### Stay Connected
- Talk to family members regularly
- Avoid isolation — baby care can feel lonely
### Manage Stress
- Do things you enjoy (music, talking)
- Don't let yourself become mentally weak
- High stress can lead to postpartum depression

## Medication & Warning Signs
### Follow Prescriptions
- Take doctor-prescribed medicines properly
### When to Consult Doctor
- Stitch infection
- Pus
- Pain
- Consult immediately if any of these occur
